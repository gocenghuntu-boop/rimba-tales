Rimba Tales

Bedtime stories from the rainforest, written and read aloud for you. For parents whose voice is tired, and kids who always want one more story.

{{ENGINE_BADGE}} {{VOICE_BADGE}}

⏳ The Lazy Payoff: tonight Rimba Tales saved you roughly 12 minutes of inventing a plot, finding a moral, and reading it out loud. Put your feet up.

Built for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 3: Slow and Steady. Stories come from an open-weight model served by Groq when configured (the app auto-selects the best model your key can access), or a local offline composer that needs no key and no network. Narration by ElevenLabs when configured, or your device's own voice. Deployed on Render.
